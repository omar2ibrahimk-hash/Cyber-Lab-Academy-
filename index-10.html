Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

1. Information and purpose

CyberLab Academy is designed for cybersecurity education. If you create an account through a connected backend, the service may process information you submit, such as your display name, email address, account credentials in securely hashed form, lesson progress, and basic security logs. Do not submit sensitive personal information.

2. Local storage

This standalone page saves lesson completion in your browser's local storage. That information remains on this browser until you clear it or reset progress. The simulated terminal does not run system commands or send its inputs to a server.

3. Use and sharing

Account data should be used to provide login, learning progress, protect the service, and troubleshoot issues. A deployed backend should explain any analytics, hosting providers, retention periods, or other sharing practices here before launch.

4. Security and children

Use a unique password and never share it. If this service is used by children, a responsible adult should review the deployed service and applicable privacy requirements. No website can guarantee absolute security.

5. Your choices and contact

You can clear local progress with the reset control or browser settings. For server-held account data, provide a verified contact address and a process for access or deletion before publishing. This template does not yet define a real operator contact.
